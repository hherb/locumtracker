# Image-generation record

- Mode: built-in `image_gen`
- Taxonomy: `logo-brand`
- Selected direction: `concepts/imagegen-direction.png`

## Prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: master app icon artwork for LocumTracker, an Australian work, earnings, receipt and rural-subsidy tracking app for locum doctors
Primary request: Create a distinctive, premium app icon centered on one bold symbol that combines a location pin, a medical cross, and a small time-tracking check accent. Make it friendly, trustworthy, and immediately readable at 16 px. The result should feel memorable and modern rather than corporate or generic.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed square emerald-to-deep-teal background with subtle dimensional depth; no transparent corners and no baked-in rounded-square mask
Subject: a large white location-pin silhouette with a clean medical cross cut into its center; a small warm golden circular clock/check badge integrated at the lower-right edge of the pin, clearly subordinate; use only essential geometry
Style/medium: polished vector-like flat icon with restrained soft depth, crisp edges, Apple-quality app icon finish, suitable for recreation as SVG
Composition/framing: centered, strong silhouette, generous platform-safe margin, balanced at 1024x1024, no thin lines, no tiny details
Color palette: emerald #087F5B, deep teal #064E3B, warm gold #F5B942, off-white #F8FAF7
Lighting/mood: calm, confident, human, subtly luminous
Constraints: no letters, no words, no typography, no watermark, no mockup device, no border, no gradients that reduce legibility, no photographic texture, do not round the outside canvas, do not use a red cross, exact square output
```

The selected bitmap was used only as a visual direction. The shipping icon and logo were redrawn as deterministic SVG geometry and exported from those vector masters.
